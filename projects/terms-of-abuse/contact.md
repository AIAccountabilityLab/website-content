## About us

**Harshvardhan Pandit** is a research fellow at the AI Accountability
Lab at Trinity College Dublin, specializing in the implications of
regulations on technology and how these solve or hinder real-world
challenges associated with privacy, legal and regulatory compliance, and
consent. His PhD (Computer Science, Trinity College Dublin) explored the
application of linked data and semantic web technologies towards GDPR
compliance, with a particular focus on consent and provenance.
