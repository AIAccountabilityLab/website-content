subtitle: "An Analysis of GenAI Services"
description: |
  We analyse the terms of generative AI services from the perspective of an EU-based consumer and share our findings that <mark>reiterate known issues</mark> as well as <mark>surface new ones unique to GenAI services</mark>. The implications of these practices are severe, as we find consumers suffer from lack of necessary information, significant imbalance of power, and have responsibilities they cannot materially fulfil without violating the terms. We make concrete recommendations for authorities and policymakers to urgently upgrade existing consumer protection mechanisms to tackle this growing issue.

## Our Contributions

We provide a [methodological framework](methodology) for performing a
fine-grained annotation and assessment of the consumer terms used by
GenAI services;

We manually annotated the terms of use of six GenAI services (see
[findings](findings)) and highlight failures regarding 'good faith',
lack of transparency, and imbalances as defined in EU consumer
protection laws (see [analysis](analysis));

We provide [concrete recommendations](recommendations) for policy makers
to both interpret existing consumer protection regulation as well as
shape new laws, to ensure better consumer protection and fairness in
GenAI services.
