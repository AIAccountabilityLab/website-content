Terms of use, or terms and conditions, are a binding legal contract
between the GenAI company and the individual consumer. Existing
literature shows terms are not read by users
[obarBiggestLieInternet2020](){.ref}, utilise legal jargon that is
difficult to comprehend [robinson2020beyond](){.ref}, and require
regulation to prevent unfair practices and ensure consumer protection
([Unfair Commercial Terms Directive (UCTD)](){.ref} and [Unfair
Commercial Practices Directive (UCPD)](){.ref}). Given that GenAI
services are a fairly new phenomenon, we focused our discussions on
those aspects which most strongly arise from the nature and use of GenAI
as a service. In this, we build on prior work that has argued the terms
are so problematic that they are \"the biggest lie on the internet\"
[obarBiggestLieInternet2020](){.ref}, with others having analysed
privacy policies of GenAI services
[taoLongitudinalMeasurementPrivacy2025](){.ref} to find specific data
collection issues, and their implications under California\'s CCPA
[king2025user](){.ref}, investigations on licensing
[leiEffectGenerativeArtificial2025](), copyright and data protection
rights [edwardsPrivateOrderingGenerative2025](){.ref}.
