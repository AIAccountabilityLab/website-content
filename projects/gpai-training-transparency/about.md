## Contact

We welcome queries, feedback, and collaborations on this work. In
particular, please do reach out to us if:

- You are a <mark>GPAI Provider</mark> who has published a public summary
  and wishes to assess its quality, or has revised their public summary
  and wants us to reassess it, or wants to know how to improve an
  already published summary.
- You are a <mark>Rights-Exercise Stakeholder</mark> who wants to understand
  how to make use of the quality assessment to understand the public
  summary.
- You are a <mark>Researcher</mark> who has done similar work or wants to do
  similar work.

To get in touch, use:

- [Github](github.com/AIAccountabilityLab/gpai-training-transparency) to
  open issues and discussions
- E-mail <aial@tcd.ie> for formal enquiries and administrative matters
- Contact **Dr. Harshvardhan Pandit** at
  [harshvardhan.pandit@adaptcentre.ie](mailto:harshvardhan.pandit@adaptcentre.ie,dick.blankvoort@ru.nl)
- Contact **Dick Blankvoort** at
  [dick.blankvoort@ru.nl](mailto:dick.blankvoort@ru.nl,harshvardhan.pandit@adaptcentre.ie)

## About us

**Harshvardhan Pandit** is a research fellow at the AI Accountability
Lab at Trinity College Dublin, specializing in the implications of
regulations on technology and how these solve or hinder real-world
challenges associated with privacy, legal and regulatory compliance, and
consent. His PhD (Computer Science, Trinity College Dublin) explored the
application of linked data and semantic web technologies towards GDPR
compliance, with a particular focus on consent and provenance.

**Dick Blankvoort** is a researcher at the European Open Source AI Index
and a former visiting researcher at the AI Accountability Lab. His work
focuses on the open-source AI and the societal implications of AI
systems.

**Maximilian Gahntz** is the AI Policy lead at Mozilla Foundation.
