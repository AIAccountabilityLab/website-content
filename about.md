# About AIAL

Purpose driven AI research. We don’t shy away from openly expressing the values that steer our research; research excellence, preservation of fundamental rights,  

Mission: ensure that the wider AI ecology – from research, product development, to governance – centres public interest, particularly those at the margins of society, often disproportionately negatively impacted. 

We firmly believe that the primary purpose of AI accountability research lies in executing rigorous research in the public interest, i.e., producing evidence in service of upholding justice, equity, the rule of law, and fundamental rights through rigours and independent investigations. 

Vision: leverage empirical evidence to inform the public, support evidence-driven AI policies, hold systems of power and authority accountable, and pave the way marked by a just and equitable AI.
